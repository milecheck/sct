Trailhead transport

4×4 shuttle · resupply

Sunshine Coast Shuttle

Runs between Saltery Bay and Sarah Point by arrangement. Hikers can ask to join an existing trip and split the cost. Resupply drops are also available by arrangement.

604-483-6374 · Provider website

Source: founder field record, July 2026. Confirm pickup point, vehicle access, price and space before travel.

Water taxi

Lund Water Taxi

Runs from Lund to the northern trailhead at Sarah Point. Sarah Point has no dock; the drop-off is on a rocky shore. Hikers sometimes arrange a shared trip to split the cost.

Provider website

Source: app field record, July–August 2026. Confirm landing conditions, price, luggage and pet rules with the operator.

Regional connections

Ferries

BC Ferries

The Earls Cove–Saltery Bay ferry reaches the southern trailhead; Comox–Powell River connects Vancouver Island. The app record lists crossings of about 50 and 85 minutes respectively. From Vancouver, the public-transport route also uses the Horseshoe Bay–Langdale ferry.

Schedules and current conditions

Source: official schedules checked 20 August 2026. Sailing times vary by date and season; use the current schedule.

Local bus

BC Transit Route 12

Some Route 12 trips connect Saltery Bay with Powell River along the Stillwater corridor. The record checked 20 August 2026 lists a $2.25 ride and $4.50 day pass. Check current payment options before boarding.

Current route, timetable and fare

Source: BC Transit pages checked 20 August 2026. Not every trip runs every day; check the current timetable and pet rules.

Vancouver connection

Sunshine Coast Connector

Connects Langdale and Earls Cove as part of the public-transport route between Vancouver and Powell River. It can be paired with Vancouver bus 257 to Horseshoe Bay.

Provider website

Source: app transport record, July–August 2026. Confirm operating dates, connections, luggage and pet rules.

Floatplane from Vancouver

Harbour Air

Flies between Coal Harbour in Vancouver and Powell River. Check the current schedule and baggage limits before building it into a trailhead connection.

Provider website

Source: app transport record, July–August 2026. Schedule, price, baggage and pet rules were not recorded.

Floatplane from Seattle

Kenmore Air

Flies from Lake Washington to Bliss Landing, about 3 km by forest road from Sarah Point. This is how the founder reached the northern end in July 2026. Fog can cancel floatplanes.

Provider website

Source: founder trip record, July 2026. She flew with an 18 lb dog; confirm the current pet, baggage and cancellation rules before booking.

Resupply and local stops

Full resupply

Powell River

The trail reaches the Shingle Mill area near marker 56. Powell River is the main resupply stop. Confirm fuel-canister and bear-spray stock with a store.

Source: founder field record, July–August 2026. Individual stores and stock were not recorded; confirm what you need before leaving town.

Food · informal handoff

Shingle Mill Pub & Bistro

On the trail at Powell River’s north edge near marker 56. Hikers have used it for a meal and an informal resupply handoff. Holding a bag is a favour, not a listed service; ask staff before relying on it.

Source: founder field record, July 2026. Confirm current hours and any bag arrangement directly.

Highway store

Lang Bay Store

A highway store near the marker 145–150 access area. Hikers arrange resupply bag drops in this area; no standing bag-drop service is recorded here.

Source: app field record, July–August 2026. Confirm current hours and arrange any handoff separately.

Maps · local information

Tourism Powell River visitor centre

On Joyce Avenue in Powell River. The app field record lists paper maps, the SCT guidebook, trail passports and local trail information.

Source: app field record, July–August 2026. Confirm current hours and stock.

Trail and emergency contacts

Official trail organization

qPAWS

qathet Parks and Wilderness Society built and maintains the trail and huts. Its site carries official hut information, trail notices and ways to support the work.

Emergency

911 and satellite SOS

Call 911 where cellular service is available. Without cellular service, use the SOS function on a satellite communicator. Give your coordinates and the reported accuracy. Report an overdue hiker at the agreed overdue time.

Source: emergency record distributed with the app. These contacts are also available in the app’s free offline safety information.
