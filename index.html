01 / What it answers

Five questions while hiking.

Core map and planning data are available offline. Community updates require a network sync.

Where am I?

Your raw GPS position and the nearest trail-marker interval. GPS continues to work in airplane mode.

Where am I planning to stop?

Huts, campsites and saved nights. Hut plans are self-reported and do not reserve space.

What is ahead?

Water in walking order, the last established source, dry camps, road crossings, wayfinding notes and sunset.

Can I get out here?

Road crossings and access records show what is known about normal cars, 4×4 vehicles, ATVs and boats. Unverified vehicle access is labelled unknown.

What have hikers reported?

Dated trail reports cover water, huts, wayfinding, wildlife, rides, lost items and trail conditions.

02 / Why another app

Doesn’t AllTrails do this?

AllTrails provides offline maps, GPS navigation and trip recording. Sunshine Coast Trail Planner adds SCT marker progress, hut-night planning, dated water reports, access records and local transport.

0–178Marker posts on trail

The route line is still being corrected.

The current line comes from OpenStreetMap and measures shorter than the walk. Progress and planning follow the numbered posts. Some intervals are longer or shorter than one kilometre because of reroutes.

A clean GPX recording from the SCT helps correct the line. Send one to sct@milecheckapp.com.

The founder

Built by Leah Gerber.

I thru-hiked the SCT in July 2026 with my dog. Planning meant cross-checking qPAWS maps, guidebooks, routing apps, transport providers and hiker reports. I built an offline app with the map, water records, huts, transport and planning details I needed.
