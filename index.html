01 / On the trail

What the app shows.

The map and saved plans work offline. Hiker reports update when the app connects.

GPS position

See your GPS position and nearest marker interval. GPS works in airplane mode.

Plan your itinerary

Plan where you'll stay each night — huts and campsites along the route — and share your plan with family.

Water sources

Check water sources along the trail and when each was last confirmed.

Hiker reports

Read reports from other hikers along the trail — water, wayfinding, trail conditions.

Rides

Coordinate rides with other hikers.

02 / What is different

Made specifically for the Sunshine Coast Trail.

AllTrails has offline maps, GPS navigation and trip recording. Sunshine Coast Trail Planner adds marker progress, hut nights, water warnings, access records and local transport.

We’re improving the trail data.

There are conflicts in the actual length of the hike and in the distances between kilometre markers — reroutes over time, logging activity, new and safer bridges.

To improve the app, we’d love a clean GPX track of your hike: sct@milecheckapp.com.

The developer

Leah Gerber

I thru-hiked the SCT with my dog in July 2026. I planned the trip with qPAWS maps, guidebooks, routing apps, transport sites and hiker reports. I built the app to keep that information together and available offline.
