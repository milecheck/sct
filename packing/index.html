Sunshine Coast Trail packing list

What I carried on a 2026 thru-hike, what worked, and what I would leave home.

I overpacked and left extra clothing at Shingle Mill. Several hikers I met did the same. This list separates what I used from what I would leave home.

Some links go to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Products are labelled as gear I carried or gear I would consider next.

SCT-specific packing decisions

◆Capacity for 3 litres of water. Carry 3 L of capacity for dry camps and the Tin Hat dry stretch.
◆Water filter and backup treatment. Water comes from lakes and creeks. Some sources are seasonal and many remain unchecked.
◆A way to store food away from bears. Some huts have a cache. Some don’t, and a cache can be full or broken when you arrive — carry your own plan.
◆A sleeping pad even if you plan on huts. The huts are first-come and free to anyone who reaches them. If one is full, you’re on the floor or in a tent.
◆Weigh the loaded pack. Mine became harder to manage once food and dry-camp water were added. Three litres of water weighs 3 kg (6.6 lb).
◆Plan food by days. Marker intervals do not equal measured walking distance after years of reroutes.

What I carried

This is one hiker’s 2026 setup. Fit, weather and route length matter more than a product link.

Pack and sleep

  • I carried a 55 L pack with a two-person tent for me and my dog. A 50–60 L pack held this setup.
  • I used a −7°C (20°F) sleeping bag, liner and inflatable pad. Hut bunks are wooden platforms.
  • A quieter, lighter pad is the first sleep item I would upgrade. Shared huts make pad noise obvious.
  • I carried a tent because huts are free, shared and first-come. Confirm camping options for each planned night.

Water and food

  • I carried containers with 3 L total capacity and filled them according to the next dry stretch.
  • Bring a filter or purifier plus a backup treatment method. Water comes from lakes, creeks and hut barrels.
  • Carry your own food-storage plan. A hut cache can be absent, full or damaged.
  • Plan food by days and include one extra day. Confirm any resupply arrangement directly before leaving.

Clothing and feet

  • Quick-dry hiking layers, dry sleep layers, a rain shell and a thin insulating layer.
  • Two or three pairs of wool socks. No sock prevents blisters; carry a kit and deal with hot spots early.
  • I wore Merrell Moab 3 waterproof mids. Use broken-in footwear that works for mud, roots and rock.
  • I used gloves in summer. A swimsuit is useful at the lakes.

Navigation and safety

  • Download offline maps before leaving signal and carry backup route information.
  • Carry a battery bank sized without relying on solar charging. Much of the trail is under canopy.
  • Carry a headlamp, first-aid kit, repair kit and your own medications.
  • Carry bear spray where you can reach it and know how to use it. Bear spray and fuel cannot travel on a commercial flight, including checked baggage.
  • A fitness watch records a hike. A satellite communicator such as an inReach can send an SOS without cellular service. Check which device and subscription you have before leaving.

Small items

  • Trekking poles, a quick-dry towel, trowel, hand sanitiser, sunscreen, insect repellent, lip balm and a hat.
  • Pack liner, dry food bag, cord, tape, blunt-tip scissors and a small sewing kit.
  • Use small amounts of biodegradable soap at least 60 m (200 ft) from lakes and streams.

Dog

  • Bring the dog’s food, water capacity, bowl, leash, sleep setup and first-aid supplies. Ask your vet about nutrition, medication and insect protection.
  • Trail Paws paw wax is the lick-safe paw protection I carried. Check paws during road and rock sections.
  • BC Transit permits only small pets in a secure hand-held carrier. Confirm dogs with every shuttle, taxi, boat and accommodation.

What I’d leave behind

Gaiters, a second cook pot, spare “town clothes”, and most of the just-in-case pile. Extra weight makes the climbs harder.

A hammock, specifically. I carried one and did not use it.

If you need to leave excess gear at Shingle Mill, confirm the arrangement with the pub first.
